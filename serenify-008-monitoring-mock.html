Live reading is smoothed, so it drifts rather than flickers. There's deliberately no big live number — a confident verdict you watch all day is itself stressful and would put the model's noise on display. The bloom carries the feel; the trend carries the proof. The same trend appears on the dashboard check-in card so you get the at-a-glance read without opening this page.
